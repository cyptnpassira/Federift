# FedeRift roadmap

Checked items are shipped and runnable; unchecked items are future work.

## Completed foundation
- [x] **M1 — Deterministic federated core.** Toy-vector clients, seeded runs, IID partitioning, FedAvg, and CLI reports.
- [x] **M2 — Non-IID and robust aggregation.** Dirichlet skew, trimmed mean, uniform mean, and simplified multi-Krum.
- [x] **M3 — Privacy diagnostics.** Clearly labelled approximate DP accounting and leakage heuristics.
- [x] **M4 — Go topology handoff.** Latency, drops, stragglers, partitions, and shared traces consumed by Python.

## Planned / out of scope today
- [ ] Production RDP/PLD privacy accounting.
- [ ] Real model training and calibrated privacy attacks.
- [ ] Congestion-aware graph network simulation.
- [ ] Automated test suites.
